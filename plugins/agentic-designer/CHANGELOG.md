# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-13 — first public release
- **Jarvis**, a god-level product designer agent that plans in ≤ 15 lines, builds real screens in Figma with your design system,
  polishes them from screenshots, and checks them with a lint script — for every industry, style, and device (incl. foldables).
- **Fresh Eyes** critic (sees only screenshots, taste, and rubric) with a cheap pixel-diff mode.
- **Pandu** for prototypes (HTML with a device viewer, or Figma) and motion (Figma, Lottie JSON, or dev spec).
- **Desi** for design systems: `/add-ds` stores your DS once; `/new-brand` and `/optimize-brand` manage brand modes safely.
- **Pram** (PRDs, research), **Wulan** (copy, localization), **Dev** (engineering advice), **Sari** (nightly learning).
- Screen naming `flow.context.state[.variant][.device]`, `/rename-screens`, lanes for exploration, flows, and DS maintenance,
  checkpoints and `/resume`, `/init`, `/doctor`, a live 3D office, a 121-check test suite.
