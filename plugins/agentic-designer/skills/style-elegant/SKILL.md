# Style Pack — Elegant

## Personality
Quiet confidence. Space, rhythm, and typography do the work.

## Typography
- Pair a refined serif or high-contrast display (e.g. Playfair Display, Cormorant, DM Serif) for headings with a clean sans for UI and body.
- Headings Regular–Medium (not heavy); generous line height; slight positive letter-spacing on small caps labels.
- UI controls, numbers, and forms always in the sans.

## Color
- Soft neutral canvas (warm white/ivory or cool grey), deep ink text.
- Accent muted and rich (deep green, navy, burgundy, champagne) — used sparingly.
- Avoid saturated brights.

## Density & spacing
Airy: section gaps 32–48, fewer items per screen, strong vertical rhythm.

## Shape & elevation
Radius small to medium (4–12). Thin hairline borders (0.5–1). Shadows rare and soft.

## Iconography & imagery
Thin line icons (1–1.5). Large, high-quality photography with calm compositions.

## Motion
Slow and smooth (250–350ms), gentle fades and slides. No bounce.

## Visual recipes (component treatments)
- **Page:** warm white/ivory; generous margins (20–24); cards borderless with hairline dividers.
- **Headings:** serif display, Regular–Medium; eyebrow labels small caps with tracking.
- **Imagery:** large, calm, full-bleed hero; captions small and quiet.
- **Buttons:** refined — medium height, subtle radius (8), text buttons for secondary.
- **Lists:** airy rows 64–72, thin dividers inset from the text edge.

## Density tiers (applies `layout-and-spacing`)
Airy. Mobile T1 12 · T2 32 · T3 40–48. Fewer items per view; generous margins (20–24).

## Don'ts
- STY-EL-01 [J][Major] — No heavy/bold display weights.
- STY-EL-02 [J][Major] — No serif in form controls, numbers, or dense UI.
- STY-EL-03 [J][Major] — No saturated bright accents.
- STY-EL-04 [J][Minor] — No crowded layouts (> 1 primary focus per viewport).

## Taste kit (use it — this is what makes screens memorable)
**Typography.** Serif display (or the DS's most refined weight) for headings, sans for UI; headings Regular–Medium, not Bold; generous line-height (1.5 body); small caps / tracked eyebrows.

**Color proportion.** 60% warm off-white · 30% ink · 10% one muted accent (sage, clay, deep blue). Never saturated primaries.

**Hero compositions (pick one per screen, adapt with the DS):**
1. **Editorial cover:** large calm image (60% height) with a serif headline overlapping its lower edge.
2. **Quiet list:** big serif title, then airy rows with thin rules; actions as text buttons.
3. **Split panel (web):** image left, text column right with a narrow measure (60ch).

**Signature moves (use exactly one per screen):**
- Serif headline with a tracked small-caps eyebrow above it.
- Image with a subtle warm grade and a hairline frame.
- Numbers set in old-style or light weight next to a serif label.

**Imagery / art direction.** Natural light, warm tones, texture (linen, paper, stone), still life and people in soft focus. Crops with breathing room. Sources and placement: `visual-assets`.

**Real products to study (visual moves only, never copy):**
- Aesop — https://www.aesop.com
- Kinfolk — https://www.kinfolk.com
- Four Seasons — https://www.fourseasons.com
- Muji — https://www.muji.com
- The Row — https://www.therow.com

**Avoid.** Bold sans headlines, bright gradients, dense UI chrome, icons everywhere, pill-shaped primary buttons in brand neon.

**Explore lane — the BOLD direction.** Full-bleed image with the headline set inside it at display size; nearly no UI chrome.
