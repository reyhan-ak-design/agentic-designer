# Media & entertainment patterns (deep)
- Home rails: continue watching/listening first, personalized, genres; one hero max, content-driven.
- Detail: artwork, title, meta (year, rating, duration/episodes), synopsis, play/resume, add to list, download, trailer, episodes list with progress, similar.
- Player: controls (play/pause, ±10s, scrub with preview), quality, subtitles/audio, speed, cast, PiP/mini-player, next episode countdown (cancellable).
- Reading/news: headline, byline, time, reading time, body with good measure, related, save, share, paywall meter.
- Subscriptions: plans comparison, trial end date, billing, manage/cancel (easy), restore purchase, family/profiles with PIN for kids.
- Events/ticketing: date/venue, seat map or categories with availability, quantity limits, queue/waiting room, e-ticket QR, transfer ticket.
- Creator tools: upload with progress, metadata, schedule, analytics summary, monetization status.
