# Style Pack — Playful

## Personality
Warm, energetic, a little cheeky — but still usable and clear.

## Typography
- Rounded or characterful sans (e.g. Nunito, Poppins, Baloo 2 for kids, Plus Jakarta Sans for teens/young adults).
- Bold headings, friendly sentence-case copy; body stays highly readable.

## Color
- Bright primary with a supporting secondary; tinted surfaces (25–100) for sections.
- Color used for wayfinding (categories), not random decoration.
- Semantic colors still reserved for meaning.

## Density & spacing
Comfortable; chunky targets; playful but aligned grid.

## Shape & elevation
Large radius (16–24), pills for chips/buttons OK. Soft, colored shadows allowed on hero elements only.

## Iconography & imagery
Filled or duotone icons; an owned illustration/mascot system if the brand has one.

## Motion
Springy micro-interactions (200–300ms) on success and selection; never blocking.

## Visual recipes (component treatments)
- **Page:** tinted sections by category color (25–100 ramp), rounded cards (20–24).
- **Type:** rounded/characterful headings Bold; body stays neutral and readable.
- **Buttons:** pill primary, springy press (handed to Pandu), friendly verbs.
- **Illustration:** brand system only, used on empty/success/onboarding, never on every card.
- **Chips & tags:** colorful but meaningful (category), max one color family per chip set.

## Density tiers (applies `layout-and-spacing`)
Comfortable. Mobile T1 12 · T2 24 · T3 32. Chunky targets; color for wayfinding only.

## Don'ts
- STY-PL-01 [J][Major] — No random rainbow colors without meaning.
- STY-PL-02 [J][Major] — No childish style for adult financial/health decisions unless the brief insists.
- STY-PL-03 [J][Major] — No readability sacrifice (fancy fonts in body text).
- STY-PL-04 [J][Minor] — No animation longer than 400ms on core flows.

## Taste kit (use it — this is what makes screens memorable)
**Typography.** Rounded or characterful display font for headings (DS font's heaviest weight if no second font), bouncy scale (hero 32–40), body stays neutral and readable.

**Color proportion.** 50% light surfaces · 30% category/brand tints (25–200 ramp) · 20% saturated brand color; 2–3 hues max, each with a meaning.

**Hero compositions (pick one per screen, adapt with the DS):**
1. **Mascot moment:** illustration or 3D character beside a big friendly headline and one pill CTA.
2. **Progress hero:** streak/level/progress ring as the centerpiece with a celebratory tint.
3. **Sticker grid:** colorful category tiles with illustrated icons, uneven but aligned.

**Signature moves (use exactly one per screen):**
- Illustrated character or 3D object reacting to state (empty, success, error).
- Chunky pill buttons with a springy press (Pandu).
- Tinted section backgrounds per category.

**Imagery / art direction.** Brand illustration system or playful 3D renders (consistent light and palette); photos only with bright, candid energy. Sources and placement: `visual-assets`.

**Real products to study (visual moves only, never copy):**
- Duolingo — https://www.duolingo.com

**Avoid.** Confetti on every action, rainbow palettes, emoji as icons, childish tone on money/error screens, mixed illustration styles.

**Explore lane — the BOLD direction.** Full-screen color field with a large character and oversized rounded type; one action.
