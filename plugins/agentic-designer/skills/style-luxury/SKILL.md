# Style Pack — Luxury

## Personality
Exclusive and restrained. Less, but richer. The product feels curated, never busy.

## Typography
- Display in a distinctive serif or wide/high-contrast sans; body in a neutral sans.
- Large type with generous tracking for short headings; small uppercase labels with wide tracking.
- Numbers precise and elegant (tabular for money).

## Color
- Deep backgrounds (black, near-black, deep navy, forest) or pure white — commit to one.
- Metallic-inspired accents (gold, champagne, bronze) as flat colors, never gradients.
- Very limited palette: 1 background, 1 text, 1 accent.

## Density & spacing
Very airy; fewer items; full-bleed imagery; strong alignment grid.

## Shape & elevation
Sharp or very small radius (0–6). No drop shadows; depth via imagery and contrast.

## Iconography & imagery
Minimal icons; editorial photography; product imagery on clean backgrounds.

## Motion
Slow, deliberate reveals (300–450ms). Parallax only on marketing surfaces.

## Visual recipes (component treatments)
- **Page:** commit to deep dark or pure white; one metallic-tone accent as flat color.
- **Headings:** wide/serif display, large, generous tracking on short uppercase labels.
- **Cards:** sharp or 0–4 radius, no shadow; separation by space and contrast.
- **Buttons:** rectangular, 48–56 tall, understated; no pills.
- **Details:** thin rules, restrained iconography, product imagery on clean backgrounds.

## Density tiers (applies `layout-and-spacing`)
Very airy. Mobile T1 16 · T2 32 · T3 48+. One focus per viewport; full-bleed imagery.

## Don'ts
- STY-LX-01 [J][Blocker] — No metallic gradients or fake gold textures.
- STY-LX-02 [J][Major] — No more than 3 colors on a screen (excluding imagery).
- STY-LX-03 [J][Major] — No discount-style badges or loud promo patterns.
- STY-LX-04 [J][Major] — No rounded pill buttons.

## Taste kit (use it — this is what makes screens memorable)
**Typography.** High-contrast display (wide sans or refined serif) at large sizes with generous tracking on short uppercase labels; body small and quiet.

**Color proportion.** Commit: 80% deep dark or pure white · 15% neutral · 5% a single metallic-tone accent as flat color.

**Hero compositions (pick one per screen, adapt with the DS):**
1. **Object hero:** one product/card image centered on a pure field, name below in tracked caps.
2. **Black card:** a dark full-bleed hero with a single thin-rule button.
3. **Gallery rail:** horizontally scrolling large images with minimal captions.

**Signature moves (use exactly one per screen):**
- Oversized tracked-caps wordmark or product name.
- Rectangular hairline buttons (no pills), lots of empty space.
- A single metallic accent line or numeral.

**Imagery / art direction.** Studio product photography, dramatic single-source light, dark or seamless backgrounds; no crowds, no lifestyle clutter. Sources and placement: `visual-assets`.
