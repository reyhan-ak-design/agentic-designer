# Healthcare & wellness patterns (deep)
- Home: next appointment card, active prescriptions/reminders, quick actions (book, consult now, lab results), family switcher.
- Booking: specialty → doctor (filters: gender, language, price, insurance/BPJS accepted, rating) → slot calendar → patient → insurance/payment → confirmation with preparation notes and add-to-calendar.
- Telemedicine: symptom intake (short), queue position & ETA, consent, call UI (connection quality, chat, share photo), post-consult summary, e-prescription, follow-up.
- Pharmacy: prescription vs OTC, upload prescription, substitution consent, delivery vs pickup, medication info (dose, timing, side effects link).
- Records: lab results (value, unit, range, flag text, trend chart), visit history, vaccination, allergies (user-entered), share with doctor (time-limited).
- Wellness: goals, streaks without guilt, habit check-ins, wearable sync status, mental-health check-ins with crisis resources visible.
- Safety: emergency CTA where symptoms indicate, clear disclaimers, human escalation.
- States: doctor unavailable, slot taken, insurance not accepted, lab pending, sync failed.
