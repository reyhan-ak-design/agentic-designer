# Anti-slop

## Directions must really differ
Explore directions differ on **≥ 3 of 5 axes**: composition/layout · typography (family or scale logic) · imagery
treatment · color strategy · density/rhythm. Same layout with a new palette = one direction, not three.

## Banned tropes (seen in our own tests — replace with a specific idea)
Marketing/websites: headline pattern "Make X. Make it Y." with a grey second line · stats row ("120+ · 9 yrs · 4.9/5") ·
"Clear steps, no surprises" 4-step process · fake client logo wall · dark testimonial card with a round portrait ·
giant faded wordmark in the footer · one blob/pill shape repeated as decoration · "Book an intro call" pill everywhere.
Apps: balance card + 4 round quick actions + list (unchanged Dribbble default) · crypto dark UI with neon green on black ·
glassmorphism on content · purple→blue gradient hero · 3D abstract blobs · emoji as icons · every card with the same shadow ·
centered everything · sparkline on every row · "Good morning, <Name> 👋" hero by default.
Copy: "seamless", "elevate", "unlock", "empower", "next-level", "all-in-one", "effortless" — say what it actually does.

## Data & instruction adherence (hard checks)
Currency, number format, language, market, and names follow the brief (e.g. **Rupiah with id-ID formatting**, not $).
Times are consistent (status bar time vs greeting vs transaction times). Realistic, local data — no Lorem, John Doe, Acme.

## Replace a trope with
A specific, ownable idea from the brand, the product's real data, or the Manager's taste images (`taste-library`).
