# Style Pack — Senior-friendly

## Personality
Patient, clear, reassuring. Nothing is hidden; nothing is tiny.

## Typography
- Highly legible sans with open shapes (e.g. Atkinson Hyperlegible, Nunito Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Body ≥ 17–18pt; labels ≥ 15pt; headings modest (clarity over drama).
- Regular and Semibold only; no light weights.

## Color
- Strong text contrast (≥ 7:1 for body where possible).
- One calm accent; semantic colors always paired with icon + text.
- No low-contrast greys for important text.

## Density & spacing
One task per screen; big spacing between targets (≥ 12); no crowded rows.

## Shape & elevation
Clear boundaries: visible borders on inputs and cards; radius medium (8–12).

## Iconography & imagery
Icons always with text labels. Literal, familiar icons.

## Motion
Minimal; no auto-advancing carousels; respect reduced motion.

## Visual recipes (component treatments)
- **Page:** high-contrast text, white cards on light tint, visible borders on inputs (1.5–2).
- **Type:** body ≥ 16–18, headings modest; labels always visible above inputs.
- **Buttons:** 52–56 tall, full-width, text + icon; never icon-only primary.
- **Lists:** 64–72 rows, big leading icons with labels, one action per row.
- **Guidance:** step indicator "Langkah 2 dari 4", helper text before errors happen.

## Density tiers (applies `layout-and-spacing`)
Comfortable-plus. Mobile T1 16 · T2 24–32 · T3 40. Body ≥ Body L 16 (prefer 18 via H5 where DS allows); targets ≥ 48.

## Don'ts
- STY-SF-01 [J][Blocker] — No icon-only buttons for primary actions.
- STY-SF-02 [J][Blocker] — No text below 15pt for meaningful content.
- STY-SF-03 [J][Major] — No gesture-only interactions (swipe to delete) without a visible alternative.
- STY-SF-04 [J][Major] — No time-limited interactions without extension.
- STY-SF-05 [J][Major] — No more than one primary decision per screen.

## Taste kit (use it — this is what makes screens memorable)
**Typography.** Body 18 (never < 16), headings modest (22–28), Semibold labels; high x-height DS font; line-height 1.5; no light weights.

**Color proportion.** Strong contrast (≥ 7:1 for body where possible) · white cards on a light tint · one clear action color · semantic colors always with icons + words.

**Hero compositions (pick one per screen, adapt with the DS):**
1. **Task list:** big title, then 3–5 large tiles with icon + label ("Kirim uang", "Bayar tagihan").
2. **One question per screen:** big question, big input, one full-width button.
3. **Status card:** plain-language status ("Pembayaran berhasil") with the key number large and a clear next step.

**Signature moves (use exactly one per screen):**
- Extra-large action tiles with labels under icons.
- Friendly helper line before errors happen.
- Step indicator in words ("Langkah 2 dari 4").

**Imagery / art direction.** Real people of the audience's age in everyday settings, warm and respectful; large, simple illustrations; avoid abstract art. Sources and placement: `visual-assets`.

**Real products to study (visual moves only, never copy):**
- GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk
- AARP — https://www.aarp.org
- Mobile JKN (BPJS Kesehatan) — https://www.bpjs-kesehatan.go.id
- WhatsApp — https://www.whatsapp.com
- Apple Assistive Access — https://www.apple.com/accessibility/

**Avoid.** Icon-only buttons, gestures as the only path, low-contrast greys, dense tables, tiny close buttons, time-limited steps.

**Explore lane — the BOLD direction.** A warm illustrated hero with the one main action as a huge tile; still plain words.
