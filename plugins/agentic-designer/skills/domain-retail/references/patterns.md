# Retail patterns (deep)
- Membership: member card (barcode/QR, brightness boost), tier progress, points balance and expiry, benefits by tier.
- Rewards: catalog of redeemable items/vouchers, point cost, redeem flow, voucher wallet (active/used/expired), terms.
- Promotions: weekly catalog, promo detail (period, channel: online/offline, participating stores, terms), add to list.
- Store locator: map/list, hours, services (pickup, parking), stock check per store.
- Shopping list / omnichannel: list → check availability → pickup or delivery → order status.
- Receipts: digital receipts after in-store purchase, points earned, returns eligibility.
- POS/staff: product search/scan, cart with quantity, discounts/member lookup, payment split, receipt, void/return with supervisor PIN, end-of-shift summary.
