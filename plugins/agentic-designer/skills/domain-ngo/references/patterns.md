# NGO & social-impact patterns (deep)
- Campaign discovery: categories (pendidikan, kesehatan, bencana, lingkungan), urgent/near target filters, verified organizer badge.
- Campaign page: story (real, dignified), progress (raised/target/donors/days), fund usage breakdown, updates timeline with photos/receipts, organizer profile + legal registration, share.
- Donate: presets + custom, recurring toggle (monthly) with clear cancel info, anonymous option, prayer/message, payment, thank-you with impact statement and share; receipt/tax letter if applicable.
- Zakat/infaq: calculator with type selection, nisab explanation, payment to chosen amil, certificate/receipt.
- Donor account: giving history, recurring management, impact reports, preferences.
- Volunteering: events list/map, requirements, sign-up, check-in QR, hours log, certificate.
- Trust: transparency reports, audited financials link, contact, complaint channel.
