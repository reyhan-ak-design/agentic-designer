# Style Pack — Corporate Trustworthy

## Personality
Reliable, orderly, competent. Nothing surprising, everything in its place.

## Typography
- Neutral, sturdy sans (Inter, IBM Plex Sans, Source Sans 3, Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Clear hierarchy, conservative sizes, Semibold headings, Regular body.

## Color
- Blue/navy/teal families or the institution's color at 500; strong neutrals.
- Accent restricted to actions and key status; lots of white/neutral surface.

## Density & spacing
Medium-dense; structured grids; consistent section headers.

## Shape & elevation
Radius small–medium (6–10). Borders over shadows. Clear section containers.

## Iconography & imagery
Outline icons, consistent weight. Imagery: real people and workplaces, not stock clichés.

## Motion
Subtle and quick (150–200ms).

## Visual recipes (component treatments)
- **Page:** white/neutral background, structured sections with clear headers, cards with 1px border radius 8–12.
- **Type:** sturdy sans, Semibold headings, generous line-height for body.
- **Data:** tables and key-value lists with right-aligned numbers; status chips subtle (tinted, not saturated).
- **Buttons:** rectangular-rounded (8), filled primary + outline secondary.
- **Trust cues:** license/regulator footer, security badges only if real.

## Density tiers (applies `layout-and-spacing`)
Medium. Mobile T1 12 · T2 20–24 · T3 32. Web: sections 32–40; tables 48 default, 40 dense.

## Don'ts
- STY-CT-01 [J][Major] — No playful illustration or mascots.
- STY-CT-02 [J][Major] — No pill-shaped primary buttons.
- STY-CT-03 [J][Major] — No trendy effects (glass, neon, gradients).
- STY-CT-04 [J][Minor] — No inconsistent section header styles.

## Taste kit (use it — this is what makes screens memorable)
**Typography.** Sturdy sans, Semibold headings, clear numeric style (tabular); hero 28–36; labels always visible; generous line-height for body.

**Color proportion.** 75% white/neutral · 15% deep brand blue/green for structure (headers, active nav) · 10% accent for actions; semantic colors subtle (tinted chips).

**Hero compositions (pick one per screen, adapt with the DS):**
1. **Account summary card:** brand-colored card with balance, account number masked, 3 quick actions.
2. **Structured dashboard:** KPI row + one chart + recent activity table.
3. **Guided form:** stepper + sectioned form with a summary panel (web).

**Signature moves (use exactly one per screen):**
- Brand-colored summary card with a subtle pattern or shape in the corner.
- Crisp key–value lists with right-aligned numbers.
- Trust footer (license/regulator line) styled quietly but present.

**Imagery / art direction.** Real people at work or real products, natural light, calm; abstract brand shapes for heroes; avoid handshake/stock clichés. Sources and placement: `visual-assets`.

**Real products to study (visual moves only, never copy):**
- Wise — https://wise.com
- BCA — https://www.bca.co.id
- IBM Carbon — https://carbondesignsystem.com
- Allianz — https://www.allianz.com
- Xero — https://www.xero.com

**Avoid.** Stock handshakes, piggy banks, over-decorated cards, playful illustration on compliance screens, low-density enterprise tables without hierarchy.

**Explore lane — the BOLD direction.** Large brand-color hero band with a bold figure and a single abstract shape; rest stays structured.
