# Government & public-service patterns (deep)
- Service catalog: search-first with synonyms (plain words, not agency names), categories by life event (punya anak, pindah domisili, usaha baru).
- Service page: what you get · who is eligible · requirements (documents) · fee (or free) · time · where/online · legal basis link · start button.
- Identity: NIK/KK entry with format checks, verification via national ID service, OTP; clear data-use consent.
- Forms: one topic per page, save & continue, autosave indicator, review page with edit links, declaration checkbox, submission receipt (reference number, PDF, QR verification).
- Tracking: status timeline with SLA, what's needed from the user, notifications, contact channel, complaint escalation.
- Payments: PNBP/retribusi via VA/QRIS with deadline and receipt.
- Accessibility & inclusion: large text, plain language, low-bandwidth mode, offline drafts, assisted-service option (visit counter/call center).
- Officer/back-office: queue of applications with SLA flags, verify documents side-by-side, approve/reject with reason templates, audit trail.
