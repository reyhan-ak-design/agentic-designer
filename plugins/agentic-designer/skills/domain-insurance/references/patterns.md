# Insurance patterns (deep) — load the section you need
## Buy / quote
- Needs finder: 3–5 questions (who is covered, age, budget, main worry) → recommended plan with the reason.
- Plan comparison: 2–3 columns max on mobile (swipeable), rows = key benefits with limits (e.g. "Rawat inap s.d. Rp500.000/hari"), highlight differences not sameness; "Lihat semua manfaat" for the long tail.
- Price: per month + per year + total for tenor; payment frequency toggle shows savings for annual.
- Add-ons (riders) as optional toggles with price delta; waiting period shown per rider.
- Application: insured person data (self/family), health declaration (yes/no questions, one per screen for sensitive ones), beneficiary, review, e-sign/consent, payment.
## Policy management
- Policy list with status chips (Aktif, Masa tenggang, Tidak aktif, Menunggu pembayaran) and next payment date.
- Policy detail: coverage summary, insured, beneficiaries, period, documents (e-policy, e-card), renew/upgrade, claim history.
- E-card: full-screen card with member number, QR/barcode for hospital, provider network link, offline available.
- Provider network: map/list of hospitals & clinics with cashless badge, distance, specialties.
## Claims
- Entry: choose claim type (rawat inap, rawat jalan, kendaraan, perjalanan) with required-documents preview before starting.
- Cashless vs reimbursement explained up front.
- Document capture: per-document guidance with example thumbnail, multi-page, quality check, "lengkapi nanti" save state.
- Bank account for reimbursement (verified name).
- Tracker: submitted → verified → assessed → approved/rejected → paid, with expected times and what the user can do at each step; rejection shows reason and appeal path.
## States & data
- Grace period, lapsed policy, pending payment, claim needing more documents, partial approval.
- Data: plan names (Proteksi Dasar/Plus/Premier), sum insured Rp50jt–Rp2M, premiums Rp49rb–Rp1,5jt/bulan, hospitals "RS Mitra Sehat", claim IDs `CLM-2609-00123`.
