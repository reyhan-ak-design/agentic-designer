# Domain Pack — Marketing sites & agencies

## Job
A visitor decides in ~5 seconds whether this is for them, then looks for proof, then acts. Every section answers one
question in that order: *What is it? · Is it for me? · Can I trust it? · What does it cost / how does it work? · What now?*

## Structure (choose, don't stack every section)
Hero → proof → offer/how it works → deep proof (case study / demo) → pricing or next step → footer.
Agencies/portfolios: work first — the hero *is* the best project. Products: the product UI or result in the hero.

## Hero strategies (pick one per page)
1. **Work-led:** full-bleed best project with a short, specific line ("Brand and app for Tabung, 1.2M users in 8 months").
2. **Product-led:** real UI at large scale, slightly angled or cropped, one clear CTA.
3. **Statement-led:** one bold, specific sentence set huge — only if the sentence is genuinely distinctive.
4. **Interactive:** a live mini-demo (handed to Pandu) when the product is best felt.

## Proof that isn't a trope
Named case studies with one hard number each · real client quotes with name, role, and company (never invented) ·
press or awards that exist · product screenshots · a short video. If the brief has no real proof, leave a clearly scoped
proof slot — never fake logos, stats, or testimonials.

## Case study page
Context → problem → approach (3–5 images with captions) → result with numbers → next project. Images do the talking.

## Pricing & conversion
2–3 plans max, recommended one marked, feature differences not feature lists; FAQ for objections; one primary CTA per
section with the same verb; forms short (name, email, one question).

## Responsive
Design 1440, 1280, and 390; content container 1200–1280; text measure 60–75 ch; hero type scales down by ~40% on mobile;
images re-crop per breakpoint (art direction), not just shrink. Apply `adaptive-devices` max widths.

## Avoid (from our own tests — see `anti-slop`)
"Make X. Make it Y." headlines · stats row · 4-step process strip · logo wall · dark testimonial card · giant footer
wordmark · repeated blob shape · "Book an intro call" pill in every section · stock people pointing at laptops.

## Recommended Style Packs
`style-bold-editorial` · `style-modern-minimal` · `style-elegant` · `style-luxury` (fashion/hospitality).
