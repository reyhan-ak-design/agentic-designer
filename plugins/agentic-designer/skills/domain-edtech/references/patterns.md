# Edtech patterns (deep)
- Onboarding: role (student/parent/teacher), grade/goal, level placement quiz (short), schedule preference.
- Home: continue learning (last lesson with progress), today's plan, streak (gentle), upcoming classes/assignments.
- Catalog: subject → course cards (level, duration, progress, instructor), filters, preview lesson.
- Lesson: video/reading with chapters, transcript, notes, speed control, offline download, next lesson CTA, ask-a-question.
- Practice/quiz: one question per screen, answer states (selected/correct/incorrect) with explanation, hint, skip, results summary with weak topics → recommended practice.
- Live class: schedule, join window, attendance, recording afterwards.
- Assignments & grades: due dates, submission (upload/text), rubric, feedback, grade history.
- Parent view: progress by subject, attendance, messages with teacher.
- Certificates with verification link.
